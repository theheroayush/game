# Apex Chess Master: Play AI — Google Play Listing

## Store listing copy

**App name:** Apex Chess Master: Play AI

**Short description:** Play chess offline, solve puzzles, explore openings, and train endgames.

**Full description:**

Play chess and practise at your own pace with Apex Chess Master: Play AI.

- Play against the computer with 10 selectable difficulty levels
- Play local pass-and-play games
- Solve tactical puzzles and try timed Puzzle Rush challenges
- Explore chess openings and replay their moves
- Practise endgame positions
- Review saved games and track your chess and tactics ratings
- Choose from board and piece themes

Core chess play and training work offline. Your game history, ratings, puzzle progress, settings, and statistics are stored on your device. No account is required.
