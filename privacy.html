Privacy Policy — Apex Chess Master: Play AI

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Apex Chess Master: Play AI is an offline chess game. This policy describes the information handled by the Android application.

Information and Storage

The app does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number. Game history, ratings, puzzle progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. The Android release manifest does not request internet access, and the app does not send this gameplay data to a server.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes the locally stored game information. Your device's operating system controls access to the app's private storage.

Permissions and Third-Party Services

The Android release manifest does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, media, or notification permissions. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Core chess and training features work offline.

Children's Privacy

The app does not require users to submit personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us using the channel below with questions about the app or this policy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version and its effective date will be posted at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project's GitHub issue page: https://github.com/theheroayush/game/issues. Please do not include passwords, chess account credentials, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.
